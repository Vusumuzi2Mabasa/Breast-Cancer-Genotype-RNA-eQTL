1. THREE COMPLEMENTARY QUESTIONS
MOFA+: discover molecular variation through unsupervised factors and loadings.
DIABLO: assess supervised TNBC versus non-TNBC signatures against simple prediction references.
cis-eQTL: test local genotype-expression associations and genotype-by-subtype interactions.
There is no MOFA versus DIABLO winner. MOFA subtype accuracy is not its primary discovery endpoint.

2. SIMULATION AND PROVENANCE
All participants, RNA counts, germline dosages, positions, LD and gene identifiers are synthetic.
There are two molecular layers: 24 germline variants in six local regions and 12 RNA genes.
There are no protein or somatic features and no real breast cancer eQTL discoveries.
Cases have simulated TNBC/non-TNBC labels; no healthy-control susceptibility GWAS was performed.
The original study remains at the source repository/commit in provenance/packaging.json.
Original model, eQTL and robustness records and example data are byte-preserved; their SHA256
index is provenance/original_numerical_sha256.json. Scientific Python models are unchanged.
R scripts differ only by removal of the v3/ path prefix for the standalone layout.
Original locks and source copies document that packaging change.

Original design: 1,600 eQTL cohorts, 80 independently generated training/test pairs,
training N240/N480 and 360 independent test cases per pair. Scenarios: null, shared eQTL,
subtype eQTL with abundance shifts, and abundance-only without eQTLs. An explicitly
documented HC3 sensitivity amendment adds 800 fresh null/abundance-only cohorts.
No participants, seeds, effects, model grids or test outcomes were changed by reframing.
Programme diagnostics and feature-selection frequencies are post hoc descriptive summaries
of already fitted models. They are not new prospectively selected simulation endpoints.

3. GENERATOR AND QC
Synthetic genotype dosage is 0/1/2 from correlated latent Gaussian binomial thresholds.
Local correlation is 0.65^distance; allele frequencies vary with idealised ancestry strata.
This is not empirical African or other reference LD. Age, cell composition, ancestry and
batch contribute to expression and/or case subtype. Negative-binomial counts have dispersion
30 and a known whole-assay library; analysis uses log2(CPM+1). Twelve selected genes do not
stand in for the full transcriptome. Full equations are in README.md and the Word report.
QC checks dosage values, dimensions, finite RNA, integer counts, libraries and MAF.
HWE summaries are within ancestry with an approximation only where expected counts >=5.
No FASTQ processing, actual variant calling, imputation or real sample-swap QC was performed.

4. MOFA DISCOVERY
Official mofapy2 with Gaussian working genotype/RNA views, training-only ancestry/batch
preparation and scaling, without subtype labels during representation fitting.
K2/4/6 is selected by masked training reconstruction. Genuine PCA and jittered PCA starts
are selected by training ELBO. All 80 fit choices remain preserved.
New summaries: budget counts and selected masked errors across 80 fits; eight fixed
first-replicate fixtures for projection reconstruction, residual H-programme subspace
capture and independent RNA-from-genotype prediction. These are descriptive diagnostics.
Projection R2 is not official posterior variance decomposition. Residual H is only part
of total molecular variation. The upper K6 was selected in 76/80 fits; larger budgets were
not evaluated here. Bootstrap factor stability, real pathway enrichment and biological
replication were not assessed.

5. DIABLO SIGNATURES
Official R mixOmics block.splsda uses genotype/RNA plus observed clinical adjustment inputs.
The clinical block is not another molecular assay. Stratified threefold training-only
validation selects components 1/2 and keepX4/8 (limited by block size).
Preparation is repeated inside each fold. Weighted centroid voting yields class labels.
Compare independent balanced accuracy with combined ridge, RNA ridge, genotype ridge and
clinical-only ridge using the same test participants and clinical information.
Do not manufacture AUC or probability calibration for hard DIABLO labels.
Feature selection frequency is across ten independently simulated cohorts per cell;
it is not bootstrap stability of the same patients. Response-block features are excluded.
Original MOFA/PCA prediction records remain in integration_evaluation.csv for provenance;
supervised_prediction_evaluation.csv contains only DIABLO and prediction references.

6. CIS eQTL INFERENCE
Per gene, jointly model its four local genotypes and four genotype-by-subtype interactions,
adjusting for age, cell composition, known ancestry and batch. The omnibus test compares
the full model to covariates plus subtype. The interaction test retains common genotype
effects in its null. Use observed design ranks for nested F degrees of freedom.
Apply BH separately across twelve omnibus tests and twelve interaction tests per cohort.
No joint FDR guarantee across families is claimed. Evaluate gene-level truth, not whether
each LD-correlated tag SNP is directly causal. No fine-mapping, colocalisation or mediation.
The ordinary F reference uses a working linear model on transformed counts. The HC3 Wald
sensitivity is approximate. Original abundance-only N480 error was 11% for F and 8% for HC3;
fresh values were 5.5% and 4.5%. HC3 also gave 8.5% and 9% in fresh complete-null N240/N480
cohorts. Neither reference is certified for confirmatory eQTL inference without further
calibration. All these limitations remain visible in figures, tables and the report.
